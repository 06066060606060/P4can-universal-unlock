full 2-bit HO overwrite · generated value is capped at HO=2

Visual Warning Rescue

WAIT + REFRACTORY · every eligible stock 0x370 RX draws a new carrier magnitude. Non-zero stock is mirrored to the opposite sign with |stock| + carrier. |stock| ≤ 0.05 Nm passes through unchanged. Primary events are 80% negative / 20% positive. Rescue cancels if the visual warning clears before its delay.
Stop Behavior
STOPPED only · event engine stays paused. STOCK CARRIER preserves stock torque + stock HO and advances only counter/checksum.

Pedal Response sends one immediate best-effort 0x334 one-shot from the freshest stock template when the selector changes, then remains a volatile drive-session override using the normal stock-follow overlay. Selecting STOCK releases it immediately. CHILL, SPORT and PERFORMANCE remain armed for the current drive and are cleared by a positively observed Park gear or by P4-CAN reboot.
